Master Thesis Research Design

Operationalizing AI-enabled Customer Experience Management: Developing an Evidence-Based Framework for AI-enabled Products

Customer Experience Management • AI-enabled Products • Innovation Management
Research Problem

Existing studies explain how Customer AI Experience is conceptualized and measured, but organizations still lack an operational approach for managing Customer Experience using empirical evidence.

Research Gap

Wang et al. (2024) focused on measurement, Li et al. (2025) on formation mechanisms, and Utami & Aimin (2026) on strategic synthesis. No study has yet integrated evaluation findings into an operational managerial process for AI-enabled Customer Experience Management.

Research Objective

To develop an Evidence-Based AI-enabled Customer Experience Management Framework that helps organizations systematically manage Customer Experience for AI-enabled Products.

Theoretical Foundation
Customer Experience Theory Customer Experience Management Customer AI Experience (Li et al., 2025) AI-enabled CX Scale (Wang et al., 2024) AI-enabled CX Management (Utami & Aimin, 2026) Voice of Customer Service-Dominant Logic Continuous Improvement

Understanding Customer Experience in AI Research Platforms: Evidence from GipsyAI Users

Ongoing

Topic: Empirical Customer Experience Evaluation on AI-enabled Research Platforms.

Research Problem: AI-enabled research platforms have grown rapidly, yet most companies only evaluate AI technical performance, while systematic Customer Experience evaluation through Customer Satisfaction, Net Promoter Score, and Voice of Customer remains limited.

Research Questions:
RQ1: How do Customer Satisfaction and Net Promoter Score describe the overall Customer Experience of GipsyAI users?
RQ2: What are the major pain points experienced by GipsyAI users?
RQ3: What product improvement opportunities emerge from users' Voice of Customer?

Contribution: To provide empirical evidence on Customer Experience for AI-enabled Research Platforms. This paper produces real-world Customer Experience evidence that will become one of the main inputs for the thesis.

Customer Survey NPS & CSAT Voice of Customer Thematic Analysis

Output: Customer Experience Profile, Customer Satisfaction, NPS, Pain Points, Improvement Opportunities

Measuring Customer Experience in AI-enabled Products: A Systematic Literature Review

Ongoing

Research Domain: Customer Experience, AI-enabled Products, Customer Experience Measurement, Systematic Literature Review

Research Background: The growth of AI-enabled Products has driven increasing research on Customer Experience. However, literature shows diverse approaches — Customer AI Experience Scale, NPS, CSAT, SUS, UEQ, CES, as well as qualitative methods like Voice of Customer and user review analysis. This diversity indicates no comprehensive methodological synthesis exists yet.

Research Objective: To systematically review the existing literature to understand how Customer Experience has been measured and evaluated in AI-enabled Products.

Research Questions:
RQ1: How is Customer Experience evaluated in AI-enabled Products?
RQ2: What evaluation methods and metrics are commonly used?
RQ3: What methodological gaps remain in Customer Experience evaluation for AI-enabled Products?

Research Scope: AI Chatbots, Generative AI, AI Research Platforms, AI Virtual Assistants, Recommendation Systems, AI Healthcare, AI Education, AI Financial Services, AI-powered Self-service Technologies, Intelligent IoT Systems

Systematic Literature Review PRISMA 2020 Scopus VOSviewer

Methodology: Descriptive Analysis • Bibliometric Mapping • Narrative Synthesis • Comparative Methodological Analysis
